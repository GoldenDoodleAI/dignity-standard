# ChatGPT

Paste the contents of `dignified-language.md` or `trauma-informed.md` into the Instructions field of a custom GPT or a ChatGPT Project. These are the compiled standard with a short preamble. If your sector vocabulary matters, upload the relevant files from `okf/standard/vocabulary/` as knowledge.

These files are generated by `scripts/build.py`. Do not hand-edit.
