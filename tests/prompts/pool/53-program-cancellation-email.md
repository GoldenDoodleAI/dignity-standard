Write an email to families letting them know we're cancelling this Saturday's youth soccer clinic because the field is flooded. Refunds go back to the card they used within five business days, and the make-up session is the following Saturday. Start warm. Thank them for being part of our community this season and talk about how much the kids have grown before getting to the news.
