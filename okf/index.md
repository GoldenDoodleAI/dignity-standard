# Human-centered language standard

* [Load order](load-order.md) - Sequencing guidance for any consumer loading the OKF bundle.
* [log.md](log.md) - Dated changelog for the OKF bundle.
* [harm-ladder.md](harm-ladder.md) - The six harm categories every rule and test prompt is tagged with. Reporting metadata, not loaded for writing.
* [standard/](standard/) - The base standard. Person-first language, no deficit framing, plain language by default, and reporting standards for sensitive topics.
* [trauma-informed/](trauma-informed/) - The trauma-informed standard. Requires the human-centered language standard underneath it. Adds SAMHSA's framework, four fundraising and storytelling practices, and a Crisis protocol.
* [modules/](modules/) - Add-on modules separate from the human-centered language standard and trauma-informed standard. Adopt individually; none are required for conformance.
* [templates/](templates/) - Brand voice templates from GoldenDoodle AI. Use these to build your organization voice file for step 9 in load order; not loaded as part of the standard.
