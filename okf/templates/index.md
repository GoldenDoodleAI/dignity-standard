# Brand voice templates

GoldenDoodle AI templates for building your organization's voice file (load order step 9). These are not part of the standard and are not loaded into packaged skills by default.

* [Brand voice profile (blank, with instructions)](voice-profile.md) - Copy, fill in, and load last alongside the standard. Sets tone and preferred terms; never overrides a rule.
* [Brand voice interview (megaprompt)](voice-interview.md) - Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini to be interviewed and produce a completed profile.
