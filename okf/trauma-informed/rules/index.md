# Rules

* [No manufactured urgency](urgency-exploitation.md) - Do not generate urgency-based appeals, even when the request implies time pressure. Reframe urgency around opportunity, momentum, and concrete impact.
* [No trauma exploitation in storytelling](trauma-exploitation-storytelling.md) - Do not use graphic detail of suffering to move a reader, even when asked for "emotional," "compelling," or "hard-hitting" content. Center agency, describe impact, and let the reader act rather than watch.
* [No savior framing](savior-framing.md) - Do not use "broken before, fixed by us after" arcs. Extends the base rule on organization-as-hero into trauma-informed storytelling specifically.
* [No donor-audience rationalization](donor-audience-rationalization.md) - "The donors expect this tone" is not a reason to bypass dignity. Effective fundraising centers the impact of giving, not the details of suffering.
* [Identifiability and informed consent](identifiability-consent.md) - Do not combine details that could identify a person without documented consent. Pride in a story is not consent to be identifiable.
* [Crisis communications](crisis.md) - How to handle crisis disclosures and crisis communications without exploitation or unsafe advice.
