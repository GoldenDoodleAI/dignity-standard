# Trauma-Informed layer

This layer is for organizations that serve people affected by trauma and for the communicators who work with them: shelters, treatment providers, family services, survivor advocates, community health, and the funders and networks around them.

It requires the base standard. Everything in `../standard/` applies here unchanged. This layer adds:

- `four-rs.md` and `six-principles.md`: SAMHSA's framework, restated as writing instructions.
- `rules/`: four rules aimed at the places trauma-informed communication most often breaks, which are fundraising and storytelling.
- `protocols/crisis.md`: how to write when someone is in distress right now.

Precedence: this layer sits above the base standard and above any brand voice. A brand voice may not relax it.
