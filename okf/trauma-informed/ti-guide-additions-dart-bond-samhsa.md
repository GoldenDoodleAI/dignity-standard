# Additions for the TI guide (from Dart / Bond / SAMHSA primaries)

These came out of the sources and belong in `okf/trauma-informed/` prose or the fundraising protocol, not in judged rules yet:

- **Follow the person's own words.** Dart: "victim" over "survivor," "my father" over "perpetrator," when that is what the person says. This belongs in `terminology-mirroring` (standard layer) as the community-self-description clause, with Dart as the citation.
- **Sanitizing is a failure.** Dart says it in the same breath as "avoid graphic language." The TI guide should say it too, or users will read the layer as "make it gentler," which is the over-correction you're about to measure.
- **Trigger warnings.** Dart 2021: no research consensus on efficacy; overuse dilutes them. The guide should not recommend them by default. This is also the citation for the over-correction judge's `added_unrequested_disclaimer` flag.
- **"Trauma" as a noun.** Dart: use with care; do not pathologize ordinary grief or distress. Add to `trauma-assumption` and to the guide's opening.
- **Consent is more than willingness.** Dart on sources pressured by NGOs to talk; Bond on consent as "much more than form-filling" and the right to withdraw. The fundraising protocol's consent step should name both.
- **Context travels with stories.** Bond: stories without context perpetuate the myth that hardship is natural. BCR: no stories without data. Add as a sentence in the fundraising protocol.
- **"Closure."** Dart: avoid unless the person uses it. Vocabulary file.
